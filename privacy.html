Privacy policy

Last updated 9 September 2026

The short version

This site has one form on it. What you type into it comes to us as an email so we can reply to you. We do not sell it, trade it, or send you anything you did not ask for.

What we collect

Only what you hand us on the contact form:

  • Your name, email address and, if you give it, your phone number.
  • The property address, if you fill that field in.
  • What you write to us.
  • Any photos of the space you attach — up to four.

The site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or tracking scripts. We do not build a profile of you or follow you to other sites.

Why we have it

To answer you, to arrange a visit if one is needed, and to follow up. If you go on to hire us, the same details are used to quote, schedule and invoice the work. That is the whole list.

Who else sees it

The form is delivered by Formspree, which passes the submission on to our inbox. Our email is hosted by our email provider. Both hold the message in the ordinary course of delivering it. Neither is given permission to use it for anything else.

Otherwise, nobody. We do not share your details with other contractors, suppliers or marketing services. We would only hand something over if the law required it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not turn into work are deleted within a year. Records for jobs we did do are kept for seven years, because tax rules say so.

Photos of your property

Photos you send us are used to understand the site and price the work. We sometimes photograph our own finished jobs and put them on this site or on Instagram. We ask first, every time, and a no costs you nothing.

Your say in it

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we will do it — no form to fill in, no reason required. Under British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act you are entitled to that, and to complain to the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for BC if we handle it badly.
